Connect your organization's tools, knowledge, and AI with LinkSense, running on your own infrastructure.

## Get started

1. [Choose a deployment](./deploy/index.mdx): compare Core and Full and check the host requirements.
2. [Complete the first setup](./deploy/first-setup.mdx): create an administrator, configure a model, and check the services.
3. [Run your first task](./user-guide/getting-started/first-task.mdx): describe your request, follow its progress, and review the results.

Already have a LinkSense instance? Start with [signing in](./user-guide/getting-started/sign-in.mdx).

## Choose a guide

- [User guide](./user-guide/overview.mdx): for everyone who creates tasks, uploads files, installs capabilities, or manages personal settings.
- [Developer guide](./developer-guide/interactive-application.mdx): for interactive application packages and external iframe integration.
- [Administrator guide](./admin-guide/overview.mdx): for user, permission, plugin, knowledge base, model, authentication, health, audit, and usage management.

Every help article is publicly readable. The pages and operations described in the administrator guide are still protected by LinkSense administrator authorization.

## Find your way

1. Browse features in the left navigation.
2. Search page titles and content with the search field or `⌘/Ctrl + K`.
3. Switch between English and Simplified Chinese from the language menu.
4. Use the page contents to jump to a section, or the LinkSense logo to return to the website.

<Callout type="info" title="Private local search">

The search index ships with the static help site. Queries and documentation content are not sent to a third-party search service.

</Callout>

## Documentation scope

These articles describe current LinkSense usage and administration features, including task context, image generation, automations, message channels, file annotations, application creation and embedding, feedback, and administrator settings.
